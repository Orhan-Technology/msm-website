## Still needs a real photo

- **I-Beams / H-Beams** — both are crops of one photograph; exactly one usable
  freely-licensed beam photo exists. Two separate shots are needed.
- **Steel Angles** and **Plate Steel** — the two images on the page with **unknown licensing**.
  For angles: It is a real
  photograph and the only one found showing the inverted-V ends that identify angle stock;
  no freely-licensed equivalent exists (searched Commons, Openverse, Pexels and Pixabay in
  English, German, French and Russian). Replace it with MSM's own photo when possible.

## Do not use

- `structural-beams.jpg` — a Naisam envelope/stationery mockup, not a product. It was
  previously wired to both I-Beams and H-Beams.
- Google Images results. Nearly all are Adobe Stock / Shutterstock / Alibaba supplier
  photos; agencies run reverse-image crawlers and bill per image found on a commercial site.
